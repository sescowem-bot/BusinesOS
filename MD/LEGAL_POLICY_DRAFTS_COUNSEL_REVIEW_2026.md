## 1.1 What BusinessOS is
BusinessOS provides web software to help businesses organise customers, sales, payments, invoices, expenses, stock, team members and related business activities. This notice describes processing undertaken by the BusinessOS operator in its own capacity. A merchant that enters information about its customers or staff generally determines the purpose of that information; the merchant's privacy notice also applies.

## 1.2 Responsibilities for personal data
For registration accounts, platform subscriptions, billing, support, fraud prevention and other purposes determined by us, [OPERATOR] ordinarily acts as data controller. For business records that merchants submit to use the service, merchants are generally the data controllers and we process those records to provide the service under their instructions. The actual contractual allocation may differ in specific circumstances. The merchant Data Processing Agreement governs processor activity. Do not assert a role without confirming the actual processing purpose.

## 1.3 Data categories
Depending on the functions used, we may process:
- Identity and account data: names, email addresses, login IDs, account/role settings and authentication event metadata.
- Merchant profile: business name, contact details, business address, authorised personnel, chosen visibility of public directory details.
- Merchant-entered records: customer contact details, transactions, invoice recipients, part payments, amounts owed, inventory and operational notes.
- Subscription/billing data: plan, invoices, payment reference/status, renewal and support records. Payment card numbers should be handled by an authorised payment gateway, not claimed to be stored by BusinessOS absent evidence.
- Technical/security data: device/browser data, timestamps, IP addresses and diagnostic logs only to the extent enabled and justified.
- Communications: customer service requests, necessary service email, and separate optional promotional communications where chosen.

**Check before publication:** actual fields, cookies, fraud prevention, marketing automation, logging, location data and all integrated providers.

**Parties:** [OPERATOR LEGAL ENTITY], as processor for **Merchant** [LEGAL NAME], as controller, to the extent BusinessOS follows merchant instructions for customer/staff/business personal data. Parties must sign/accept an approved version with effective date and version evidence.

## 4.1 Subject matter and instructions
Processor processes personal data solely to provide the contracted hosted BusinessOS services and documented merchant instructions, except where legally compelled to do otherwise. The merchant remains responsible for providing notices, identifying a valid lawful basis and accuracy of third-party data entered in the service.

## 4.2 Categories and purposes
Data subjects: merchant customers, suppliers, employees/team members and transaction contacts.
Data types: contact details, order/invoice and payment records, debtor balances, associated support data and other merchant-entered fields **after verified product inventory**.
Processing: receive, store, retrieve, display to authorised merchant staff, calculate, export, back up, delete and support the service according to instructions.

## 4.3 Confidentiality and security
Processor will restrict access to authorised personnel bound by confidentiality and maintain proportionate technical/organisational controls, including tenant isolation, access controls, secure transmission, incident procedures and controlled backups. A separate schedule must define tested measures and exceptions accurately.

## 4.4 Subprocessors and international transfers
Processor maintains a current register, requires written obligations from authorised subprocessors, provides a lawful mechanism for notification/objection to significant changes and evaluates foreign transfers under NDPA/GAID. List real vendors and their functions in a schedule, not assumed locations.

## 4.5 Rights and regulator assistance
Processor will assist merchant reasonably with access, corrections, deletion, restriction, portability, impact assessments, breach handling and regulatory enquiries within defined contractual response arrangements. Requests concerning platform-controller account data are handled under the operator's own Privacy Notice.

## 4.6 Security incidents
Processor informs merchant without undue delay on becoming aware of a confirmed or suspected personal-data breach impacting merchant records, with available scope, mitigation and ongoing findings. Parties allocate assessment, notification and affected-person communication duties; legally binding NDPC deadlines remain applicable. Do not wait for a completed forensic report before escalating.

## 4.7 End-of-service return and deletion
Set exact export window, erasure schedule, lawful retention exceptions, backup expiry and verification method in an agreed schedule. Data needed for statutory records or legal claims may be restricted and retained only where applicable law permits or requires.

## 4.8 Audit and evidence
Processor provides proportionate evidence and cooperates with legally required audits or regulator requests subject to security/confidentiality safeguards. Neither party claims a government compliance certification without documented verification.

## 4.9 Precedence
For merchant personal-data processing, the signed DPA should control over conflicting ordinary commercial terms to the extent required by applicable data protection law. Governing law, liability allocation and dispute terms require Nigerian counsel.

# 5. Operator policies that must exist outside the website
These are internal operating documents, not all public web pages:
1. **Data Retention Schedule:** table of record classes, basis, trigger, retention, deletion method, backup expiry, legal holds.
2. **Data Subject Request Procedure:** verify requester, classify controller vs processor, assign owner, acknowledge, respond, retain evidence, escalate to NDPC when appropriate.

5. **Vendor and Cross-Border Data Register:** vendor, contract, roles, data categories, subprocessors, country/transfer mechanism, renewal.

8. **Pricing, Cancellation and Refund Rules:** reflect real provider payment and subscription behavior, transparent purchase agreement and applicable consumer remedies.
