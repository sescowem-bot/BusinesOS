# BusinessOS subscription & cash-collection pitch checklist

- Demonstrate an actual Free workspace creating customers, products and orders, recording a verified partial payment and printing/downloading a customer document.
- Show the business-specific Outstanding & instalments page and agree a schedule. Verify it does not count as money received.
- Compare Starter, Growth and Professional **only after** the System Owner has approved the distinct feature matrix and set monthly/annual numeric prices. Until then, existing screenshots showing two identical plan tiers must not be presented as corrected.
- Show owner-managed subscription pricing, authorised module permissions and manual plan exceptions, without opening a client's confidential transactions from the System Owner account.
- Show a staged verified Paystack payment, if configured. Do not describe pending gateway setup as a live subscription purchase.
- Show an email reminder only if Resend delivery has been tested, and distinguish a pending renewal from an unpaid debt.
- Discuss capabilities still in development in a separate roadmap, not inside misleading customer product cards.
